Crispy Doom Data Folder

Place your IWADs here (choose one you are legally allowed to distribute/use):
  - freedoom1.wad (Ultimate Doom-compatible, free)
  - freedoom2.wad (Doom II-compatible, free)
  - doom1.wad (shareware)
  - DOOM.WAD / DOOM2.WAD (commercial – do not distribute without rights)

This folder is installed at runtime to your device's writable App Data directory:
  <app_data_dir>/crispy-doom/iwads

Your settings live in:
  <app_data_dir>/crispy-doom/crispy-doom.cfg

If no IWAD is present, the engine will prompt for one at first run or fail to start.
